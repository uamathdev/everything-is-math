Activity: Click any seed (dot) to place the pencil center.
Then click nearby seeds to draw lines from the center through them.
The cross ratio of up to 4 lines is computed and shown.

To find a harmonic pencil: Click a seed, then use Auto to see the two spiral directions and their bisectors — always CR = −1 by construction.

Or click two seeds, then Add bisectors.

Additive pencil (Fibonacci mechanism): Click two seeds to define vectors L and R from the anchor, then click Add L+R and L+2R. The four lines {L, R, L+R, L+2R} always give CR = −1 — but L+R is not the angle bisector when the two seeds are at different distances from the anchor.

Note on CR values: CR = −1, 2, and ½ are all the same harmonic pencil — just with lines in a different order.

Fibonacci neighbor pencil: With two seed lines chosen, click Add Fibonacci neighbors to add the previous and next Fibonacci family (e.g. Fₛ, Fₜ, F₝, F₞). CR ≈ −1 — improving for larger families.

Compare all three pencils: bisector, additive (L+R), and Fibonacci neighbors.
